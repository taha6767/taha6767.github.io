Get to know me!

Hey! I'm Taha Disbudak, a Computer Science student at Pomona College in Claremont, CA (Class of 2028). I spend my summers back home in Pittsburgh, PA with my family. I work at the intersection of data engineering and applied AI — building the pipelines that move and clean data, and the LLM-powered tools that make sense of it.

Most recently I was a Data Engineering Intern at TD Bank, where I built an LLM-powered extraction agent and refactored enterprise PySpark pipelines. Before that I spent a summer in Pomona's Human-Computer Interaction Lab, analyzing 230,000+ log entries from a baby-tracking app — work that became a co-authored ACM publication.

I also led TagSafe, a five-person team building a trademark-safety tool for e-commerce sellers. I like problems where messy real-world data meets a decision someone actually has to make. Feel free to get in touch.
